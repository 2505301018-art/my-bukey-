history_edu Heritage & Provenance

The Harmony of Roasted Aromas & Fresh Petals

Born from a quiet corner of the Marais in Paris and reborn in the verdant heart of Mumbai, BLOOM & BREW was founded on a simple revelation: both the master barista and the haute floriste are artisans of fleeting perfection.

Each morning at 05:30 AM, while our head roaster calibrates the water temperature and dial-in profiles of microlot Ethiopian beans, our floral team gathers stems fresh from cold-chain arrivals. We do not place flowers in our café as afterthoughts; our café exists inside an operating botanical atelier.

When you sit by our zinc counters or beneath our jasmine arbor, the warmth of dark chocolate notes interlaces naturally with the cool, green sweetness of opening peonies. Here, time loses its urgency.
