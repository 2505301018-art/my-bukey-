Frequently Inquired Courtesies

Can I customize my table's fresh floral centerpiece? yard

Yes. When selecting the 'Damask Rose Bouquet' or 'Bespoke Floral Celebration' add-on, our resident floral maestro will coordinate with you via WhatsApp to handpick the rose varieties, ribbon monograms, and colour palette before you arrive.

Are private photo sessions allowed in the Orangerie? photo_camera

Personal, discreet mobile photography at your reserved table is warmly welcomed. Professional editorial equipment, external tripods, and wardrobe changes require a private conservatory buyout prior to salon opening hours.

How do you handle dietary allergies and preferences? nutrition

All patisserie and high tea savouries are prepared fresh in our open boulangerie kitchen. Simply check your dietary preferences in Step 4, and our executive pastry chef will tailor bespoke tiered stands exclusively for your party.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy? event_repeat

We offer full refunds or seamless reservation rescheduling up to 6 hours before your reserved seating. Due to the fresh harvesting of heirloom roses, bespoke floral centerpieces are non-refundable within 4 hours of arrival.
